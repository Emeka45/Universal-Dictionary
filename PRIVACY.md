# Universal Dictionary Privacy Notice

Universal Dictionary is designed to keep personal vocabulary data on the device.

## Data stored locally
- Saved words
- Search history
- Learning streak and lookup count
- App preferences needed for reminders

This information is stored locally with Android DataStore and is not sent to a Universal Dictionary account.

## Network lookups
When a word is not available in the app's offline core, the app may contact the Free Dictionary API. Typing suggestions may contact Datamuse. The word being searched is therefore sent to those services when network lookup is used.

## Audio
Pronunciation audio supplied by a dictionary response may be fetched from its supplied URL when the user requests playback.

## Notifications
Daily vocabulary reminders are optional. Android notification permission is requested before reminders are enabled.

## No proprietary dictionary credentials
The app does not embed Oxford, Cambridge, Collins, Merriam-Webster or WordReference credentials or proprietary dictionary databases.

## Contact
For project questions, use the GitHub repository issue tracker.
